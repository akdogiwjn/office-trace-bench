L100@P2: Because the estimates presented in this report are based on a sample survey, they contain sampling
L101@P2: error and nonsampling error.
L102@P2: Sampling error is the difference between the estimate and the result that would be obtained from a
L103@P2: complete enumeration of the sampling frame conducted under the same survey conditions. This error
L104@P2: occurs because only a subset of the entire sampling frame is measured in a sample survey. Standard
L105@P2: errors and coefficients of variation (CV), as given in Table 3 of this report, are estimated measures of
L106@P2: sampling variation.
L107@P2: The margin of sampling error, as used on page 1, gives a range about the estimate which is a 90 percent
L108@P2: confidence interval. If, for example, the percent change estimate is +1.2 percent and its estimated
L109@P2: standard error is 0.9 percent, then the margin of sampling error is ±1.753 x 0.9 percent or ±1.6 percent,
L110@P2: and the 90 percent confidence interval is –0.4 percent to +2.8 percent. If the interval contains 0, then
L111@P2: one does not have sufficient evidence to conclude at the 90 percent confidence level that the change is
L112@P2: different from zero and therefore the change is not statistically significant. Estimated changes shown in
L113@P2: the text are statistically significant unless otherwise noted. For a monthly total, the median estimated
L114@P2: coefficient of variation is given. The resulting confidence interval is the estimated value ±1.753 x CV x
L115@P2: (the estimated monthly total). The Census Bureau recommends that individuals using estimates in this
L116@P2: report incorporate this information into their analyses, as sampling error could affect the conclusions
L117@P2: drawn from the estimates.
L118@P2: Nonsampling error encompasses all other factors that contribute to the total error of a sample survey
L119@P2: estimate. This type of error can occur because of nonresponse, insufficient coverage of the universe of
L120@P2: retail businesses, mistakes in the recording and coding of data, and other errors of collection, response,
L121@P2: coverage, or processing. Although nonsampling error is not measured directly, the Census Bureau
L122@P2: employs quality control procedures throughout the process to minimize this type of error.
